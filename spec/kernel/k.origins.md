# k.origins: Declared third-party origins
status: implemented

**Purpose.** A deployable names, with a reason, each other origin its documents load from.

**Declaration.** Any deployable in `topology.ts` may set `origins`, read statically, as `{ img: { 'https://img.cdn.example': 'photos' } }`: keys from `img`, `media`, `font`, `connect`, `frame`, each mapping an exact origin to its reason. An exact origin is its own serialization: scheme, lowercase host, a port only when not the default, no path, `/`, query, fragment, credentials or `*`; the scheme is `https`, or `http` with host `localhost`; no IPv6 literal. A reason is 1 to 200 characters on one line, one visible. A directive holds at most 16 origins. Visible: outside k.schema's blank set; no control or format character. No `origins`, `origins: {}` and an empty directive are the same declaration: none.

**Policy.** Each policy k.security emits for the deployable gains its origins in declaration order, nothing else: `img`, `font` and `connect` append theirs to `img-src`, `font-src` and `connect-src` after `'self'` (in `connect-src` after the `keel-peer` origins; one equal to a peer once, in its place); `media` and `frame` add `media-src` then `frame-src` directly after `connect-src`, holding the declared origins alone. So do a `node` document in production and dev (Report-Only), `csp.txt`, the `client` `index.html` a `host` serves (the `client`'s origins, not the host's), and for a shell, from its `client`: Tauri's `tauri.conf.json` (`ipc: http://ipc.localhost` last in `connect-src`), Capacitor's meta policy (`csp.txt` less what a meta tag ignores). A response that is not a document keeps `default-src 'none'; frame-ancestors 'none'`. All read the static read's declaration: the entries pass it as `createApp({ origins })`.

**Absolute.** No other directive or Trusted Types takes one; scripts are not declarable (`'strict-dynamic'` ignores host lists).

**Frame.** `Frame` from `@keel/ui` renders `<iframe>` with `src`, a required `title` (E1802, blank too), `allow` from `autoplay`, `encrypted-media`, `fullscreen`, `picture-in-picture` (joined `; `), `loading="lazy"`, `referrerpolicy="strict-origin"` and `sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-popups allow-presentation"`; it takes `id`, `lang` and the style props (k.ui). A `src` not absolute `http` or `https`, relative too, or of the document's origin renders `about:blank`. A `frame` origin of the app's own is E1590 in `keel build` (a `KEEL_PEER_<NAME>`, `KEEL_ORIGIN`) and `createApp` (`origin`, a URL peer); a document drops its own from `frame-src` (dev: one E1590 logged).

**Rules.** E1570 and E1571 (k.security) do not report a URL whose origin is declared under its directive on each deployable listing the module that holds the file or a file importing it, directly or not (none: every deployable). Importing follows k.boundary Following. They read a URL as written: a string literal, a template's text before its first substitution, the left side of `+`, `new URL` and `new Request`, through `const`s and imports; one computed otherwise is not reported. The policy, not the check, blocks an undeclared origin: a URL the check misses fails in the browser with a `csp` report (k.security Reports), never past the policy. Directives: `img` for `Image src`, `src` and `srcset` of `img`, `srcset` of `source`, `poster`; `media` for `src` of `video`, `audio`, `source`, `track`; `frame` for `Frame src` and `iframe src`; `connect` for E1571. A `srcset` is one E1570 if any absolute candidate is undeclared. `data`, `action` and `formaction` stay E1570. E1570 reports a literal `Frame src` that is not absolute, and reads `\` as `/` (`/\host`) · E1590 an `origins` the static read cannot take (not an object literal, a spread or computed key), a key outside the five, an origin that is not exact (fix: the key as its `originOf` serialization when that is exact; none for a path, as `https://cdn.example/img`), a reason out of form, or a directive over 16 (check, build, dev; `topology()` throws `Error('E1590 <deployable>: ...')`) · E1591 one origin twice in one directive (fix: delete the second entry). So is a directive twice; the first is read.

**Conformance.**
- With no `origins`, and with `origins: {}`, a fixture's production and dev documents, `csp.txt` and shell policies are byte-equal to k.security's.
- `img: { 'https://a.example': 'r', 'https://b.example': 'r' }` on a `node` deployable: its documents carry `img-src 'self' https://a.example https://b.example`, the rest as k.security; its JSON responses are unchanged.
- `media` and `frame` declared: `media-src` then `frame-src` directly follow `connect-src`, neither holding `'self'`.
- A `client` deployable with `connect: { 'https://api.test': 'r', 'https://c.example': 'r' }` and `KEEL_PEER_API=https://api.test`: `csp.txt` has `connect-src 'self' https://api.test https://c.example`; its `host` serves it with that policy; the host's own documents lack `https://c.example`.
- A Tauri shell of a deployable declaring `connect` has `connect-src 'self' <origins> ipc: http://ipc.localhost`; `csp.txt`, a Capacitor meta policy's source before any tool runs, holds a declared `frame-src`.
- E1590: `https://*.cdn.example`, `https://cdn.example/img`, `http://cdn.example`, `script: {...}`, reason `''`, a reason with a line break, one of 201 characters, `origins: shared`, 17 origins; `https://cdn.example/`, `https://CDN.example` and `https://cdn.example:443` with the fix `https://cdn.example`; `http://localhost:9000` and `https://cdn.example:8443` are none; `topology()` throws E1590 for `https://cdn.example/`.
- One key twice in `img` is one E1591 without types; one origin under `img` and `font` is none.
- `Image src="https://a.example/x.png"` in a module of a deployable declaring `img` `https://a.example` is no E1570; of one that does not, or under `frame` only, it is; `action="https://a.example/"` stays E1570.
- `ctx.net.fetch(new Request('https://c.example/e'))` in a client-reachable file is no E1571 with `connect` declaring `https://c.example`, and E1571 with it under `img` only.
- `Frame src="https://v.example/e/1" title="Demo"` renders the attributes above; without `title`, or with `title=" "`, E1802; `src="javascript:x"` renders `about:blank`; `src="/e/1"` is E1570.
- `security.json` lists each declared origin with directive and reason in order; `origins: []` with none.
- `Image src="https://a.example/x.png"` in `lib/pic.tsx`, outside every module, imported by `modules/a/Page.tsx` as `../../lib/pic.tsx` and by `modules/b/Page.tsx` as `p/lib/pic.tsx` (the project's own name), with only `a`'s deployable declaring `img` `https://a.example`, is one E1570.
