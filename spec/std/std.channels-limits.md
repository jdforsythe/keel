# std.channels-limits: Channel stream caps
status: implemented
How many channel streams (std.channels) a process holds open, and what an open past a cap gets.

**Config.** A module's `std['@keel/std-channels']: { perCaller?, perAnonymous?, maxStreams? }` (server-only; each a positive integer; defaults 8, 64 and 1,000) bounds the streams open in the process. An open is counted once `member` admits it and holds its place until its stream ends. It counts as its `user` (k.user: what the identity source answers, or a verified `x-keel-user`; `""` without identity or sign-in) when that is not `""`, else as its caller (k.server); it is anonymous when both are `""`. An open on a channel route of serving module `m` is full when:
- `m`'s channel routes hold `m`'s `maxStreams` open streams or more; else
- it is anonymous and the channel routes of every module in the process (every module served over the same server ctx; the generated entry has one per process) together hold `m`'s `perAnonymous` anonymous streams or more; else
- it has a user and that user's streams on `m`'s channel routes number `m`'s `perCaller` or more, whatever their callers; else
- it has no user and its caller's streams with no user on `m`'s channel routes number `m`'s `perCaller` or more.

A full open gets `{ kind: 'closed', reason: 'full' }` as its only event, then its end; the streams open before it continue, and `subscribe` reopens 1 s later, doubling to 30 s (std.channels Client). An anonymous stream counts against `perAnonymous` and `maxStreams`, never `perCaller`; a stream with a user or a caller counts against that user's or caller's `perCaller` and `maxStreams`, never `perAnonymous`. A route whose `member` refuses `""` holds no anonymous stream; an open `member` refuses gets `closed` `denied` and is not counted.

**What the caps bound.** The process's open anonymous channel streams number at most the largest `perAnonymous` of its serving modules (64 when every module keeps the default), whatever the number of modules or client addresses. Keel counts no address, so a client that rotates addresses shares that one count and gets no more. The anonymous places are shared: while a flood holds them, every other anonymous open, a real visitor's among them, is full. The flood takes at most that many places of a module's `maxStreams` and of `createApp({ maxStreams })` (k.server: 1,000 by default for every live loader of the app together, past it 503 `keel.busy`); streams with a user or a caller keep the rest. A signed-in person, with or without a caller cookie, has their own `perCaller` streams per module, which no anonymous flood and no other user takes; a client cannot assert a user, since only the identity source or a signed `x-keel-user` gives one. An app without identity has no user, and every stream counts by caller.

**Limits.** A caller cookie is set on any write (k.server), so a client that makes writes holds a new caller per cookie, with `perCaller` streams on each module (one signed in holds only its user's); only each module's `maxStreams` and `createApp({ maxStreams })` bound it, and while it holds the app's pool every event-stream open in the process, a signed-in person's included, is 503 until its streams end. Live loaders other than channel routes are counted only by `createApp({ maxStreams })`. Limiting opens per client address belongs to the proxy or platform in front of the app.

**Conformance.**
- With modules `a` and `b` serving channel routes at the default caps and 40 cookieless streams open on `a`'s route and 24 on `b`'s, a cookieless open on `a`'s route and one on `b`'s each get `{ kind: 'closed', reason: 'full' }` as their only event and the 64 streams continue; once one of the 64 ends, the next cookieless open on either route is served.
- With `a` at `perAnonymous: 4`, `b` at `perAnonymous: 10` and 4 cookieless streams open on `b`'s route, a cookieless open on `a`'s route is full and one on `b`'s is served; with 10 cookieless streams open across both routes, a cookieless open on `b`'s route is full.
- With `a` and `b` at the default caps and 64 cookieless streams open across their routes, a caller with a cookie is served 8 opens on `a`'s route and 8 on `b`'s and its ninth on either is full; an open whose cookie does not verify is full; and a cookieless open on a route whose `member` refuses `""` gets `{ kind: 'closed', reason: 'denied' }` as its only event.
- For every sequence of up to 200 opens and ends on the channel routes of one to three modules, each with `perCaller`, `perAnonymous` and `maxStreams` generated from 1 to 12, by cookieless callers and up to four callers with cookies, every `member` admitting every caller, an open is served exactly when Config finds it not full, and a full open's only event is `{ kind: 'closed', reason: 'full' }`.
