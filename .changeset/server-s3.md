`createApp({ runners, peers, origin })`: the served deployable's modules' `Runner`s run once after startup (a rejected `run` reruns 1,000 ms later on `ctx.clock`); `app.close()` aborts their signal and open event streams and resolves when every `run` has settled; `routes` is optional. `peers` names the other `node` deployables by URL or as in-process apps: `service({ module })` calls resolve through them, and a request for a path a peer serves (by `routes.json` in `artifacts`) is forwarded once (`x-keel-forwarded`) to that peer's origin, never to a host in the path (a path starting `//` or `/\` is not forwarded): hop-by-hop headers stay behind, the body is capped by `maxBodyBytes` (413), the peer's response, a redirect with its `location` and `set-cookie` too, is returned as is, an unreachable peer is 502 `keel.unexpected`, and each forwarded request writes its `req` log line. `origin` replaces the request's origin in `url`, the same-site check and `Secure`. Live loaders: a GET with `accept: text/event-stream` streams one `data:` line per yield, its `stores` open until the stream ends, and a `:` heartbeat per 15 s of `ctx.clock` (guard denial 403 `keel.denied`; a non-live route 406); E1558 for a live loader in a non-`node` deployable. New `@keel/server/start` (production entry: `DeployEnv`, `KEEL_PEER_<NAME>` an absolute http(s) URL, E1560, `@hono/node-server`, SIGTERM/SIGINT) and `@keel/server/dom` (`installServerDom`). Exports the `Runner` type.
