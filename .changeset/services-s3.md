`service({ module, schema })` calls an app module's routes, resolved per call: in process or through a peer on a ctx a `createApp` bound, the document's origin in a browser, else `http://<module>.keel.invalid`. An `events: true` GET endpoint returns an `AsyncIterable` of results read from a live loader's event stream (no reconnect, no resume). A client method takes `signal`.
