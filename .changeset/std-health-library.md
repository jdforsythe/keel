`@keel/std-health` is a library (k.library): the `keel` export conditions, its `@keel/*` imports as peers, and the pack scripts. Its entries are `.`, `./Health` (`src/Health.schema.ts`), `./routes`, `./contract` and `./module`. Its contract is the one file its `./contract` entry names, and `HealthView`, the route's component, keeps the id `health.status` (k.library Ids).
