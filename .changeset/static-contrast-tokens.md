`keel check`, the check `keel build` runs, `keel ledger`, `keel evidence`, `keel mcp` and `keel lsp` give each project the `tokens` export of its `tokens.ts` (`projectTokens` from `@keel/compiler/hosting/projectTokens`, evaluated fresh on every run), so static E1804, E1809 and the token-set E1801 run; a `tokens.ts` that gives no tokens is E1807 at `tokens.ts` 1:1. Static E1804 resolves `fg`, `bg`, `fontSize` and `weight` as the render check does, through parents in the component, and names the ratio needed. `check()` takes `projectArtifacts` (k.a11y-static).
