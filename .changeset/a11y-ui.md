`contrastTable(tokens)` lists every ordered pair of distinct color tokens per scheme as `{ scheme, fg, bg, ratio }`, the WCAG ratio to two decimals (k.a11y). `Input`, `TextArea` and `Select` take `autocomplete: Autofill`, a closed union of the WCAG 2.2 input purposes plus `off`, written as the `autocomplete` attribute. `brandTokens.textSecondary` is `#6B6B74`: 5.01:1 on `surface`, 4.75:1 on `surfaceLow`.
