The document's `<title>` is the matched route's `title`, the deployable's name without one, after a loader `err` and for a 404; `<html lang>` is the route's prefix param value, `en` otherwise (k.head). A prefix param's default segment answers 308 to the URL without it (k.prefix). A guard redirect carries the matched route's same-name params; a missing one is E1508 (500 with a diag).
