`keel scaffold project` writes a `.gitignore` for every project. `keel build` reports every artifact it is responsible for, written or unchanged, so the count is the same on each build. The generated `server.mjs` prints `ok listening on port N`; a port it cannot bind is one E1560 line and exit 1; W1557 names an https `KEEL_ORIGIN` as the fix. A forwarded peer response drops hop-by-hop, `connection`-named, `content-encoding` and `content-length` headers. A route `title` receives `data` typed as its loader's `output` value.
