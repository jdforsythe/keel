`Part` from `@keel/ui/aria` is for app widgets too and takes the k.roles roles: `grid` and `gridcell`, `tree` and `treeitem`, `group` with `list`, `abbr` on `columnheader`, and the native controls `slider` (`input type=range`), `radiogroup` and `radio` (`input type=radio`). `@keel/ui` exports the host element `Host`, `hostCode` and `customElement`, with the types `HostCode`, `HostOut` and `HostProps` (k.host-element, k.host-code).
