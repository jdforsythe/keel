`service({ maxBytes })`, on a `contract` or a `base` service: the most of a response body a call reads, default 1 MiB. A longer 2xx body is `api.decode`, a longer other body `api.status` with `body` `null`. Neither service follows a redirect: a 3xx is `api.status`. A path param `''`, `.` or `..` is `api.input` at the param and nothing is sent.
