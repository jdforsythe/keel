A database error of class `22`, `40` (but the reruns), `53`, `54`, `55`, `57` or `P0` is `postgres.database` with data `{ sqlstate }` and nothing of the error's text (`DatabaseError`, in `StatementError`); `57P01` to `57P03` and class `08` are `keel.unreachable`; any other SQLSTATE is the defect E2461. `follow` whose listening connection does not open, or closes before it listens, calls `on({ kind: 'lost' })` once and resolves a stop function instead of rejecting.
